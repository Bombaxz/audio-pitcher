Privacy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

The app

Audio Pitcher has no accounts and collects no personal data. Audio is processed on your PC and is never sent to any server. Your settings (theme, colors, favorite apps) are stored only on your computer.

The app keeps a local diagnostic log (app version, Windows version and the names of your audio devices). It never leaves your PC unless you use Report a problem, which saves a copy on your Desktop for you to send.

When it opens, the app checks GitHub's public API to see whether a new version is available. That request includes no data about you beyond what any internet connection exposes, such as your IP address.

This website

This site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and understand how the page is used. They may use cookies; visitors in Europe are asked first. There are no ads. You can block or delete cookies from your browser.

The installer download is served by GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic request data (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The Ko-fi donation panel only loads if you open it, and is then covered by Ko-fi's own privacy policy; Cafecito links take you to their site.

Contact

For any questions, open an issue on GitHub.
